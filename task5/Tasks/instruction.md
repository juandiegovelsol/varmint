# Bar stops keeping up while you point at it

**Describe the bug**
Point at the bar in the screenshot and nothing it draws moves on. The circled tooltip keeps
whatever it had when it opened, and the marks along the bar keep whatever they had.

**Expected behavior**
While the bar is on screen everything it draws stays current: the tooltip's contents, and how both
the tooltip and the marks are picked out once the points they stand for are reached. Pointing
somewhere else on the bar describes that point instead.
