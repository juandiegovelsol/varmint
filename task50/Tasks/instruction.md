# Screen does not communicate what was sent

When a withdrawal finishes, the screen should clearly show what happened on both sides — what was actually sent as well as what was received — regardless of how the amount was entered or whether the payout was unwrapped. If the information needed to work out what was sent isn't available yet, show the amount as entered rather than leaving it blank.
