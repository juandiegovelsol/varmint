# A tree's leafy top is detached from its trunk, with a gap of sky between them

### What happens
In `recording.mp4`, a tree on a grassy hillside is broken apart. From 0:00 to 0:11 its dark, leafy
canopy sits on the sloped ground by itself, with no trunk under it. From 0:13 to 0:18 the trunk is
in view, but the canopy hangs away from its top, with open sky in the gap between them.

### Expected
The leafy canopy should sit directly on top of its own trunk, matching up with it and forming one
normal-looking tree, with no gap of sky or ground showing between the trunk and the leaves.

### Environment
All.
