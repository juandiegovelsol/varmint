# A field's caption doesn't match what it reports

One of the fields in the panel shown in the attached screenshot carries a
caption that the product uses elsewhere, for a concept that has nothing to do
with what the field is displaying here. As it stands, a reader cannot tell from
the caption what that field actually reports. Restating what kind of value sits
in the field would not help either — the caption has to name the run this panel
is about.

The values displayed in the panel are correct and must be preserved exactly as
they are. The change must not be observable anywhere else in the product.
