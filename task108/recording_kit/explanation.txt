The prompt names no file, function, field, filter chip or amount; the only date it gives is the column header (26-05-03) of the clicked table cell. It says only that a weekly deposits Custom Report, its stacked bar chart and the transaction list opened from it are wrong, shows six screenshots of the real app (table, stacked bar chart and the opened transaction list, each as it is today and as it should be), and states general requirements: a click on a category or Transfers amount opens the transactions behind it, keeping the report's filters, in every view (bar, stacked bar, line, donut incl. the two-ring Category+Group donut, table) and for payment reports too; the rows and numbers must be right for payment reports too; anything not described (e.g. what a Net report lists and opens for its other rows) keeps behaving as today; a row's label does not identify it (translated, and ordinary categories may share it). Every concrete defect is legible only in the screenshots. The media were re-shot for this hardening from this task's own source (dev build of the web app in headless Chromium, local budget seeded through the app's own RPC), "current" with every defect present and "expected" with the reference fix, same budget and same saved report.

Upstream PR #8001 fixes the single-ring donut only in Category+Group mode: a Category-split donut slice stays broken after the upstream patch, so the oracle also threads it there.
