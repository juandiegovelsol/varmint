# Badge sheets print incomplete badges

An organiser prints attendee badges several to a sheet. Each of these badges also carries its own coloured strip and supporter mark when printed on its own page. On the sheet, every slot prints an incomplete badge, as shown in the attached screenshot — even the one slot that still shows a fragment of what should be printed is not right either. The export offers more than one arrangement of badges to a sheet, and badge designs aren't all the same shape, yet each one still has to fit its slot correctly on every arrangement.
